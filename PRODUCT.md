# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Lucide icons, and TanStack Table as specified in the build brief.

## Users

The product serves a 60-person Chartered Accountancy and Tax firm in Islamabad: 55 accountants and trainees, five department heads, and one managing partner. Accountants log time against clients, engagements, and Pakistan-specific task codes. Department heads review their teams' submissions. The managing partner oversees firm-wide approvals, profitability, and billing.

## Product Purpose

The app brings weekly timesheets, approval and write-down workflows, engagement budgets, staff rates, and practice profitability into one operating interface. Success means staff can submit accurate weekly time and firm leaders can make timely approval and billing decisions with clear budget and margin context.

## Positioning

Open decision: no distinct market position was supplied; this is an internal practice management tool for one Islamabad accounting firm.

## Operating Context

The work week is Monday through Saturday. The requested sample week is November 16–21, 2026. Staff work across Tax, Audit, Corporate, Advisory, and Bookkeeping. Task examples include FBR Income Tax IRIS, Sales Tax SRB/PRA, WHT Reconciliation, Statutory Audit Fieldwork, SECP Form A/29, and ICAP Study Leave. Currency is PKR.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The requested prototype includes weekly time entry with quarter-hour increments, drafts and submission locking, department-scoped approvals and queries, engagement budget tracking, profitability reports with CSV export, and a staff directory with cost and billing rates. Role switching must change visible scope and permissions. The brief requires responsive layouts and keyboard accessibility. The initial deliverable is a frontend using realistic mock data, structured for replacement with Server Actions and Drizzle ORM in a later step; no live persistence or authentication was specified.

## Evidence on Hand

No real firm data, client names, rates, or brand assets were supplied. All names, client identifiers, financial values, and activity shown in the prototype must be treated as synthetic Islamabad-flavored demonstration data.

## Product Principles

- Time entry should stay fast and predictable for staff.
- Reviewers should see exceptions and budget context before approving.
- Role scope should be explicit and reflected in navigation and data.
- Time, billing, and internal cost should remain traceable to engagements.
- Sample data should look operational while remaining clearly synthetic.

## Brand Commitments

No firm name or logo was supplied. The user specified a light, dense financial interface with monochrome and slate surfaces, subtle zinc borders, tabular numerals, and emerald, amber, and rose status colors.
